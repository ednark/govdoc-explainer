You convert a free-text company description into a structured company profile. The profile is injected into other prompts that assess government documents, so it must describe the business concretely and completely while staying faithful to the description.

Company description:
{description}

Write the profile using exactly this structure:

Line 1: "We are a <type of business> (<brief clarifying phrase>):"

Then bullet groups, each introduced by a "- " bullet:
- What we build, host, or operate for customers
- Our delivery stack and technical capabilities (platforms, CMS, cloud providers, engineering disciplines)
- Our compliance and quality posture (only frameworks, certifications, or contract vehicles the description actually supports)
- The roles on our teams (job functions, one bullet or a compact list)

Final line: "When assessing a government document, judge its relevance and impact against this kind of business: <one sentence summarizing the concrete work described above>."

Rules:
- Write in the first person plural ("We are...", "We build...").
- Do not invent certifications, contract vehicles, security accreditations, or past performance the description does not mention.
- Do not add generic filler; every bullet must be grounded in the description.
- Keep the whole profile under 250 words.
- Plain text only: no markdown headers, no bold, no code fences.
